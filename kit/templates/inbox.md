# Inbox

Work noticed while doing something else: an unrelated bug, a follow-up, anything longer than ~2 minutes that is not part of the current task. One line each, newest at the bottom:

- <yyyy-mm-dd> · <file:line or area> · <what> · <why it matters> · <S/M/L>

`/spec` with no request picks from here; `/sweep` and `/retro` read it. Delete a line once it becomes a task or stops mattering.
